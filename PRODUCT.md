# PROJECT BLACKSITE product brief

PROJECT BLACKSITE is an original, static browser FPS for the 404 Game Jam. Its
core loop is infiltrate, complete a tactical order, collect evidence, survive
enemy pressure, and extract. The campaign follows the AEGIS blackout across
four procedural environments and four operation types. Desktop and touch play
share one runtime and the public `window.__READY__` / `window.__GAME__`
competition contract.
