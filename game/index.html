FIELD GUIDE // ECHO ONE

SURVIVE THE
BLACKOUT

A coordinated paramilitary force is collecting fragments of the AEGIS command protocol. Track the signal through four regions and uncover who controls the cell.

  1. Move and take cover. WASD or the left touch stick. Sprint with Shift. Jump with Space or JUMP.
  2. Fight. Mouse or drag to aim, fire with click/FIRE, ADS with RMB/AIM, reload with R/RLD, and switch with 1–6 or SWAP.
  3. Use a terminal or relay. Follow the objective marker, walk close to its screen, then press E or tap USE. Blacksite and refinery consoles also activate when you approach closely. Watch for the objective to change; if it says REACH EXTRACTION, follow the beacon to finish.
  4. Complete the order. Recover intelligence, eliminate hostiles, survive hold windows, defeat Commanders, and extract.
  5. Adapt. M or the MAP button opens the tactical map. ESC/MENU opens pause, restart, respawn, settings, and mission selection.
