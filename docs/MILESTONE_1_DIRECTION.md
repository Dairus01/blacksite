## Direction contract

### World

BLACKSITE is a hardened communications compound interpreted through night-flight-line material discipline: charcoal anti-slip ground, concrete equipment shelters, blue-gray steel, olive tactical equipment, warm guidance lamps, and cold maintenance light. Surface detail comes from construction joints, frames, rails, conduit, vents, fasteners, cable trays, and recessed panels rather than noisy decals.

### First viewport

The player sees a dark road leading through a lit security threshold toward the operations structure. The route is immediately legible, the facility has a silhouette beyond the gate, and neither the view weapon nor HUD hides the destination. The title screen uses that same live view with the combat actors withheld.

### Player path

The slice moves through authored thresholds: insertion road, security gate, checkpoint crossfire, operations entrance, service stair, upper deck, and extraction. Each threshold changes enclosure, light, and cover density while preserving one readable objective.

### Signature interaction

The hostile’s silhouette visibly changes with state: low-ready patrol, shouldered aim, firing recoil, hit disruption, and physical collapse. The player weapon mirrors that mechanical clarity through a centered sight picture, receiver movement, magazine removal, and restrained recoil.

### Cross-surface reach and honest risk

Desktop can carry peripheral environment and wider status spacing. On 390 × 844, the scene keeps a clear central combat column while large controls move to the lower corners and secondary radio/HUD information yields space. The honest risk is draw-call pressure: detailed procedural kits must share materials and use instancing to stay below the measured 400-draw target.
