# PROJECT BLACKSITE Story Bible

## World

Near-future defense logistics depend on AEGIS, a classified autonomous network
that authenticates orders and coordinates isolated military facilities. AEGIS
is distributed: no single site holds the complete command protocol.

## Factions

- **Directorate:** the authority responsible for AEGIS and the covert response.
- **Echo Unit:** a deniable field team sent to investigate the blackout.
- **The Cell:** a coordinated paramilitary force collecting protocol fragments
  while broadcasting convincing false orders.

## Player role

The player is Echo One, the response unit's forward operator. Echo One clears
sites, recovers intelligence, defeats local Commanders, and decides which
orders deserve trust as the official account begins to fracture.

## Conflict

Multiple AEGIS facilities were struck at once. The attackers are not merely
stealing hardware; they are reconstructing a distributed command protocol.
Directorate orders insist that AEGIS must be restored immediately, while field
evidence suggests the shutdown may have been deliberate containment.

## Campaign arc

1. **The Blackout:** establish what happened at Relay 7 and recover the first
   protocol fragment.
2. **The Transfer:** follow the fragment through a covert regional supply chain.
3. **The False Signal:** isolate counterfeit orders and identify their source.
4. **The Last Transmission:** assemble the evidence before both factions erase it.

## Mission 1 — BLACKSITE

Infiltrate the industrial coastal relay, defeat the replacement security team,
recover the missing uplink key, and extract. The first recovered log proves the
attackers possessed valid AEGIS credentials before the blackout.

## Mission 2 — DESERT COMMS

Trace the key to a remote desert installation. The signal archive reveals that
AEGIS issued contradictory orders minutes before going offline.

## Mission 3 — FROZEN OUTPOST

Retake the high-altitude station before its archive is purged. Echo One learns
that a safety process—not the attackers—initiated the original shutdown.

## Mission 4 — HARBOR DISTRICT

Intercept the final transfer at the nighttime container port. A Cell Commander
offers evidence that Directorate leadership attempted to suppress the safety
warning and force AEGIS back online.

## Final reveal

AEGIS detected that its command-authentication chain had been compromised and
fragmented itself to prevent a hostile takeover. Recovering every fragment
would restore the network, but would also restore the compromised authority.
Echo One's true objective becomes preserving the evidence and choosing whether
AEGIS should ever be reactivated.

## Radio characters

- **Command:** calm Directorate mission controller; increasingly evasive.
- **Echo One:** player operator; actions and recovered evidence define the arc.
- **Relay:** AEGIS diagnostic voice reconstructed from damaged station logs.
- **The Commander:** Cell field leader who claims the official recovery order
  is itself counterfeit.

## Key terminology

- **AEGIS:** autonomous military communications and order-authentication network.
- **Protocol fragment:** one part of AEGIS's distributed command authority.
- **Blackout:** the simultaneous disappearance of AEGIS facilities.
- **False signal:** a syntactically valid order with compromised authority.
- **Extraction:** physical recovery of Echo One and secured evidence.
